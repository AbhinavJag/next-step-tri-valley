    Train instead of just searching

    Under California Training Benefits, you can keep getting unemployment while you're in approved training, and you don't have to look for work while you're enrolled. Ask EDD before your 16th week of benefits to qualify for extra weeks. EDD doesn't pay tuition, so pair it with a fee waiver or a training scholarship from the Tri-Valley Career Center.
