    How much would unemployment pay me?

    California pays based on your best three-month stretch of pay in the past year and a half. This gives you a quick estimate using EDD's own benefit table. EDD makes the final call.

    Want to train instead of just searching?

    Under California Training Benefits, you can keep getting unemployment while you're in approved training, and you don't have to look for work while you're enrolled. You have to ask EDD before your 16th week of benefits to qualify for extra weeks. EDD doesn't pay tuition, so pair it with a fee waiver or a training scholarship from the Tri-Valley Career Center.
